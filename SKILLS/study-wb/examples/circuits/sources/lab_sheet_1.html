Lab sheet 1 - DC circuits

Invented example source for study-wb.

  1. A 4 Ω resistor is connected to a 12 V battery. Find the current and the power the resistor dissipates.
  2. Resistors of 100 Ω, 220 Ω and 330 Ω are in series across 9 V. Find the total resistance, the current in mA, and the voltage across the 220 Ω resistor.
  3. A 6 Ω and a 3 Ω resistor are in parallel across 12 V. Find the equivalent resistance, the total current, and the current in each branch.
