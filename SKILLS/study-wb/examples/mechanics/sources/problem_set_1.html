Problem set 1 - Motion, force, energy

Invented example source for study-wb. Use g = 9.81 m/s².

  1. A cart starts from rest and accelerates at 2.5 m/s² for 8 s. Find its final speed and the distance covered.
  2. A 1,200 kg car accelerates at 3 m/s². Find the net force, and the car's weight.
  3. A 2 kg ball is held 15 m above the ground. Find its potential energy, and its speed just before landing if it is dropped (no air resistance).
